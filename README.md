## 🧭 The problem
Plant parents don't kill plants on purpose — they just forget. One plant wants water every four days, another every three weeks, and they're scattered across five rooms. Keeping it all in your head is how the fiddle leaf fig ends up crispy.

## 💡 What Sprout does
Sprout is a calm, **local-first** plant-care companion built around one question: *what needs me today?*

- **A "Today" garden** that tells you exactly which plants are thirsty — water them right from the home screen.
- **Per-plant care** — watering interval, light, humidity, difficulty, a live soil-moisture meter, care tips and your own notes.
- **A care schedule** that buckets every plant by when it's due (now / tomorrow / this week / later) and re-sorts the instant you tap 💧.
- **Watering history** logged for every plant.

Everything lives privately in your browser. No account, no notifications you'll mute.

## 🛠️ Tech
React 19 · Vite 8 · Tailwind CSS 3 · **Schibsted Grotesk** + Inter · real plant photography · localStorage with a date-based care engine · GitHub Pages.

Art-directed around a forest-green, warm-oat & terracotta palette — a quiet garden journal, not a utility app.
